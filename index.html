The Frustration of Renting in the Philippines

Scattered & Unverified Listings

Endless scrolling through unorganized Facebook groups, outdated websites, and duplicate marketplace posts. It's hard to know what's real and what's not.

Vague, Unclear Details

Is it pet-friendly? How close is it to the train station? Landlord rules, association dues, and other critical details are often missing, leaving you guessing.

A Stressful, Time-Wasting Process

The entire process is inefficient. You waste hours messaging unresponsive posters and viewing places that don't fit your needs, leading to a tiring and untrustworthy experience.

Our Solution: Clarity, Structure, and Trust

Cribb PH was built to fix the broken rental process. We provide a centralized, renter-first platform that gives you the control and information you need.

A Single, Reliable Source

No more hunting across different platforms. Cribb PH gathers listings in one place, offering a structured and easy-to-navigate database for residential rentals nationwide.

Listings You Can Actually Use

We work with landlords to ensure listings are complete and verified. Get clear details on amenities, building rules, pricing, and location from the start.

Find a Home That Fits Your Lifestyle

Our powerful, lifestyle-centric filters help you find exactly what you're looking for, whether it's a pet-friendly condo, a place near public transport, or a home with specific accessibility features.
